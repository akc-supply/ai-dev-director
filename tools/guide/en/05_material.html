BOM Management

Sidebar › Operations › BOM ManagementAccess: BOM Management

Here you look up and correct which materials, and how many of each, make up a finished product (BOM), and at what price materials are bought (purchase price).

Top tabWhat it does

Find · correct materials

Step by step · BOM (bill of materials)
  1. Click the BOM (bill of materials) tab.

    The first time, a progress bar runs while the data downloads. After that, it opens right away.

  2. Enter a part number · part name · process name, etc. in the search box.

    Narrow down further with Type · Level · Customer. To clear all, click Reset. Results are split into pages of 50.

  3. Click a row to open the BOM detail specs window.

    Parent · child information, process · production specs, notes. Click the customer name to see the business partner information.

  4. To correct it: Edit → change the values → Save.
  5. If needed, download the currently filtered list with Excel.

    File name: BOM자재명세서_날짜.xlsx (BOM bill of materials_date)

  • If you correct the child unit (UM), it is remembered separately for that part number and kept even when the BOM is uploaded again. It is shown in orange text in the table. If you empty the field, it goes back to the original unit.
  • Level 1 has a green tag, level 2 a blue tag. Required quantity = the number needed for one parent item.
Delete in the detail window removes that row from the database completely. Before deleting, check the parent · child part numbers once more.

Purchase price · Product material cost

  • Purchase price list: search box (item code · item name · vendor name · spec) + vendor · currency · type selectors → within the results, narrow down once more with Abbreviation · Item kind. Details → price information → Edit → Save. Excel downloads the narrowed list as is.
  • M-BOM (product material cost): expand a main category (project) and choose a product to see the total material cost to make one. Adjust prices in a different unit with Apply price formula (× 10, ÷ 100, etc.); this is kept even after re-uploading. To remove it, click Remove formula.

Upload BOM · price files

Step by step · Upload the BOM
  1. Click Settings at the top right of BOM Management → go to data upload.

    This is Settings inside BOM Management, not Settings › Upload on the main screen.

  2. For the BOM, upload one Frm_MASTER BOM file in BOM upload.
  3. For prices: Select finished-product file · Select semi-finished file → Upload prices (merge finished + semi-finished).

    Get the template with Template. Only the uploaded kinds are replaced, and child units you changed by hand are kept.

Use the two switches on the right of the detail header to take a material out of alerts · orders, or put it back. When you switch, the screen, automatic email and AI search recalculate with the same judgment.

Step by step · Set dormant stock yourself
  1. Open the material detail and look at the 💤 Dormant stock switch on the right of the header.

    At the end of the switch, Auto = the system's judgment, Manual = set by someone. Hover over it to see the automatic judgment and the reason (example: 0 usage in the last 30 days · 0 demand in the next 60 days), and who set it · when.

  2. Turn the switch on or off.

    On shows Marked as dormant stock; off shows Changed back to non-dormant stock. Materials you set as dormant show as 💤 Dormant (set manually) in the dashboard table.

  3. To go back to the automatic judgment, switch it back to the same side as the automatic judgment.

    The manual setting is cleared and the end of the switch shows Auto again.

Step by step · Stop after run-out
  1. Turn on the Stop after run-out (no reorder) switch on the right of the detail header.

    Marked as discontinued after run-out appears, the status becomes Discontinued after run-out, and the order cell in the list shows No reorder.

  2. To make it an order target again, turn the switch off.

    Discontinued-after-run-out mark removed — it is an order alert target again appears.

Planned receipt · Confirm receipt · Set stock count

Under View details at the bottom of the detail, click the 📦 Receipts · Stock records card. The fields for a new record are at the top, and the record list is below.

Step by step · Register a planned receipt (when you placed an order)
  1. From the kind buttons, choose 📦 Planned receipt.
  2. Enter Planned date · Qty, and write the PO number · shipping date, etc. in Memo (optional).
  3. Check the preview line below.

    If registered → expected run-out A → B (+N days) · reorder date C. If the receipt comes after the run-out, a ⚠ warning appears.

  4. Click + Register.

    The material is now marked 📦 Ordered, and a waiting-for-receipt bar appears at the top of the detail.

Step by step · Confirm receipt (when it actually arrived)
  1. Click ✓ Confirm receipt on the planned row (or on the bar at the top).
  2. Check or correct Actual date · Actual qty.

    The quantity that will actually be added to stock is previewed below. If there is an MES purchase within 7 days before the receipt date, ⚠ There is an MES purchase within 7 days before the receipt date appears. If that MES is a different receipt, choose Different receipt (adds the whole confirmed quantity); if it is the same receipt, choose Same receipt (adds only what MES has not already counted).

  3. Click Add to stock.

    To add a receipt directly without a plan: kind ✅ Confirm receipt → date (up to today) · quantity → + Add to stock.

Step by step · Set stock count (when you actually counted)
  1. Choose the kind 🔢 Set stock count.
  2. Enter Count date and Actual quantity on hand (0 is allowed).

    The preview shows the difference from the calculation.

  3. Click + Set stock count.

    From that day, counting starts again from this quantity.

  • Correct a record: Edit → correct it in the fields below → Save. Delete: Delete → confirm. To turn a confirmed receipt back into a plan, click Back to plan.
  • Saving shows on the screen right away, and the server save continues in the background. If it fails, Could not save, so the change was undone appears.

Supplier shipping record (planned departure · shipment confirmed)

For each planned receipt, record the planned departure date the supplier gave you and the day it actually left (shipment confirmed), so you can see at a glance whether goods are on the way. The planned receipt date and the stock calculation do not change.

Step by step · Record a shipment
  1. In the detail's 📦 Receipts · Stock records, click 🚚 Shipping record on a planned receipt line.

    The 🚚 Supplier shipment fields open under the line. If it is already recorded, the button shows the current state (example: 🗓 Departs 11/20); click it to change.

  2. Enter the Planned departure date from the supplier and click Save.

    The state becomes 🗓 Departs MM/DD.

  3. When it actually leaves, enter Actual departure · Tracking no. · B/L (optional) and click Save.

    If it left today, just click 🚚 Shipped today. The state becomes 🚚 Shipped MM/DD. If you made a mistake, use Delete record.

MarkMeaning · what to do
🗓 Departs MM/DDDeparture date from the supplier · not yet confirmed as shipped
🚚 Shipped MM/DDActually left (shipment confirmed) · hover to see the tracking number · days from departure to receipt
⚠ Departure MM/DD passedThe planned departure date has passed with no shipment confirmation — check with the supplier whether it was actually sent

Where it shows: next to the 1st · 2nd receipt dates in the material list · the waiting-for-receipt bar at the top of the detail · the stock run-out chart (▶ departure date · departure → receipt line) · the order recommendation cell in Dashboard › Stock · Runout table · the ordered line in the automatic email.

PO Planning · Send SL to suppliers

Materials › PO Planning

Enter the monthly order plan (SL) by material and send it to suppliers by Excel · email. FIRM = 4 months including this month, FORECAST = the 6 months after that. Example: in November, FIRM is November–February and FORECAST is March–August. When sending to suppliers, choose the period to send (up to 12 months) and the language (Korean / English).

Step by step · Enter the order plan
  1. Narrow down the materials with the filters.

    Search · View from (10 months from that month) · item group (default ★ Focus items) · supplier · type · customer · With plan only.

  2. Click ✎ Edit.
  3. Enter quantities in the month fields.

    Faded numbers are the expected usage for that month. ≈ marks an estimate from the average of the last 3 months, for months after the plan · S/L ends. Enter = field below, Tab = next field, Esc = undo that field.

  4. Click Save (N).

    All changed fields are saved together. To stop, click Cancel (it asks whether to undo everything). Changed fields are kept even if you move to another page.

Auto-fill · Clear range · Memo

  • ⚡ Auto-fill: Which materials (whole screen / this page / materials with a plan) · Which months (FIRM · FORECAST · 10 months) · Which cells (empty only / overwrite) · fill with 3-month average · Include VINFAST backorder (% by month, total 100%) → Fill. It is not saved yet, so you must click Save (↶ Undo is possible).
  • 🧹 Clear range: choose materials · months to clear only the quantities (memos stay). This also needs Save to take effect.
  • 📝 Memo: material memo · memo per month · edit history. A cell with an orange dot has a memo for that month.

Make a request (requester)

Step by step · New free issue request
  1. Click + New request.

    The + New free issue request window opens. Put several part numbers into one request.

  2. At the top, enter Requester · Division · Vehicle · Sample needed date · Purpose.

    e.g. Purpose: KGM development validation sample · parts for JG rework

  3. On each line, choose Material/Product and enter the Part No..

    Enter just the part number and the name and unit fill in automatically and lock (✓ match). If the part number is unknown, ✗ Not in the item list and similar part numbers appear — pick one.

  4. Enter the Requested qty, and if needed the Issue type (preferred) and Remarks.

    Leave the issue type empty and Production Control will decide (warehouse stock · production request · receipt request). Add lines with + Add line; remove one with ✕ at the end of the line.

  5. Click Submit request.

    N request lines registered — waiting for Production Control. appears and the status is Waiting. If a part number exists nowhere, you are asked Submit as is / Fix part no..

  • Your own Waiting requests made on screen can be changed or removed with Edit · Delete on the line. After Production Control confirms, only they can change it.
  • Click a line to open the detail panel on the right: progress ① request → ② confirm → ③ issued, request details, Production Control handling, Materials stock and expected run-out for the same part, and the log (who · when).
  • To see only your own, tick Only my items · my requests.

Confirm · issue (Production Control)

Only people whose account department contains '생산관리' (Production Control) or '생관', and masters, can do this. You have the right if the header shows ✓ Production Control access.

Step by step · Confirm
  1. Click ✓ Confirm on a Waiting line.

    For several lines, tick the boxes on the left and use ✓ Confirm on the bar above. Ticking a group header selects the whole request.

  2. Choose the Method: warehouse stock · production request · receipt request.
  3. Enter the Available date, and for materials still to arrive, the Expected receipt date.

    For several lines, (Apply to all) fills the same date. Add a Memo if needed.

  4. On each line, check the Planned receipt qty and the Materials planned receipt Add switch.

    With Add on, it is added as a planned receipt (🎁 mark) for the same part in Materials. It is on by default for receipt requests. Part numbers not in the Materials list (finished goods etc.) show Not in list.

  5. Click ✓ Confirm.

    The status changes to Confirmed · in progress. To change dates, use Edit on the line.

Step by step · Issue
  1. Click 📦 Issue on an in-progress line.

    To issue a Waiting line straight away, use 📦 Issue now in the detail panel; for several lines, tick them and use 📦 Issued on the bar above.

  2. Enter the Issue date (up to today) and Issue place (warehouse · line).
  3. On each line, check the Issued qty and Deduct from stock.

    If left on, the issued quantity is entered as a minus in Materials Receipts · Stock records on the issue date, and stock and expected run-out change at once. If the quantity is text such as “1 BOX”, only a record is kept and nothing is deducted (enter a number to deduct).

  4. Click 📦 Issued.

    The status becomes Issued and the table's Issue column shows Stock −N.

  • Reject: Reject → reason (required) → Reject. To take it back, use Undo reject in the detail panel.
  • Undo (detail panel): Cancel confirmation = back to Waiting (removes the planned receipt that was added automatically · receipts already confirmed stay) · Cancel issue = back to in progress (removes the deduction, so the stock comes back).
Delete in the detail panel also removes the linked Materials planned receipt and stock record, and cannot be undone.

Step by step · Set up automatic email
  1. Turn on the Auto send switch at the very top.
  2. In ①, set the schedule switch · time · Every day or Select days for each group.

    ◉ marks the group used for preview · test · send now. The same group is sent only once a day. If there is nothing to send, it is logged as Skipped.

  3. In ②, choose the tables to include.
