Frequently asked questions

When you are stuck, look here first. Click a title to open the answer.

Login · Screens

I cannot see a menu

You have no access to that screen. Nothing is broken. Ask an admin (Master) for the screens you need. → Roles and access

I forgot my password

On the login screen, click Forgot your password? and enter your ID · name · employee number. A temporary password (valid for 30 minutes) appears. → Forgot password

The verification code email does not arrive

Check your spam folder, and after 1 minute click Resend code. If the email address in your account is wrong, ask an admin to correct it.

I was logged out while working

If you do nothing for 30 minutes, you are logged out automatically. Click Keep using in the window that appears 2 minutes before, and you can continue. The installed Android app and the VF Shipment Management screen (08:00–19:00) are exceptions.

The numbers are still old · I cannot see data uploaded by someone else

Data updates by itself, but if you are typing in an input field, it changes after you finish. If it still does not change, press F5 (refresh). In Materials, click Recalculate.

A downloaded Excel file shows a format warning

Click Yes and it opens normally. If you save it under another name, it becomes a normal Excel file.

Stock · Shipments

Stock looks lower than the real quantity

Available Stock subtracts the quantity of pending invoices that have not left yet. If the goods have already left, click Ship in VF Shipment Management. The deduction is released.

This month's target (S/L) does not change

This is normal. This month's target is fixed by the last S/L round of the previous month, so it does not change in the middle of the month. The backorder uses the latest round.

I cannot upload the same invoice again

Add a higher Rev number to the file name, such as _Rev01. A same or lower Rev is not added. → Upload invoices

An invoice is not shown in Shipment Management

Check that it was saved in INV Issue, or that the Excel file was uploaded to Available Stock. Also check that View is Waiting, and that no region · transport · search condition is set.

The alert sound does not play

Browsers block sound until you tap the screen once. Tap the screen once and try 🔊 Sound test. If it shows 🔕 Alerts off, click it to turn alerts on. → Alerts

I uploaded the wrong file

For shipment data · production actuals, keep Replace that month only turned on and upload only that month's file again. For a weekly S/L, upload it again with the same date and Overwrite, or delete it with Delete S/L snapshot.

Some part numbers show an amount of 0

These part numbers have no unit price registered. Add the part numbers from the yellow bar in Upload › Price Management.

Materials · Email · Other

I don't see the Confirm · Issue buttons in Free Issue Requests

Confirm, issue and reject are only for people whose account department contains '생산관리' (Production Control) or '생관', and masters. Others can only request and view. Excel upload is for masters only. → Confirm · issue

A material in use shows as dormant (or the opposite)

Change it yourself with the 💤 Dormant stock switch in the material detail header. When off, it is subject to out-of-stock · order alerts again. → Dormant stock · Stop after run-out

Korean text remains in the English SL email

Click 🌐 AI English translation in the email window or fix it in English yourself. If AI translation is not available, the Korean stays as it is, so read it before sending. → Send an English SL

There is a planned receipt, so why does it say 'Receipt check needed'?

A plan is not an actual receipt. If the material is normal only when the plan arrives, or the planned date has passed, check whether the goods actually arrived and click ✓ Confirm receipt. If it is already in MES, clear it with In MES.

Can I send again right away when the email result is unclear?

First check the status · recipients · time in the send log. If it shows Partly not received, you only need to check the rejected addresses. Decide whether to send again after looking at the log and any bounce emails.

The AI answer seems wrong

Check the numbers on the screen itself using the shortcut below the answer, and click 🚩 Report answer. An admin refines it in AI Training.

Glossary

Words you often see on the screens.

Orders · Plans

TermMeaning
S/LShipping List. The planned quantities the customer sends, saying when and how much they want. Received anew every week.
Round (snapshot)The order on the day an S/L was uploaded, kept as one snapshot. Called by its date, like 07-21_SL.
RollingLooking ahead over many months from one round.
BackorderOverdue quantity that should have been delivered but has not been yet.
FIRM · FORECAST · PASTConfirmed · forecast orders (order plan) · past months. The material order plan is FIRM 4 months + FORECAST 6 months; the SL sent to suppliers is up to 12 months.
Adjustment ratioThe share of the S/L expected to actually sell (usually about 80%).
FYFiscal year. From April to March of the next year.

Stock · Shipments

TermMeaning
Stock count · Actual stockQuantity counted directly in the warehouse · current quantity after adding production and subtracting shipments since the count.
Available stockQuantity you can really ship (Sea: + planned production, Air: − packing pending).
ShippableAvailable stock minus pending invoices.
S/L RemainingThe part of this month's target not yet filled.
RevRevision number of an invoice corrected and issued again. Higher = newer.
Pickup Request · BlockedA promise: "please ship on this day with this truck" · a mark: "this pallet cannot be shipped".
P/L No · NET · GROSSPallet serial number · weight of the goods only · weight including packing.
Forwarder · DGTransport agent · Dangerous Goods (airbag explosives, etc.).

Materials

TermMeaning
Lead timeNumber of days from order to receipt. Can be edited with ✎ in the material detail.
Free issueGoods sent out free of charge, such as samples and take-outs. In Free Issue Requests: request → Production Control confirms → issued.
DormantA material with no usage in the last 30 days · next 60 days. You can set or undo it yourself with the 💤 switch in the detail.
Stop after run-out (discontinued after run-out)A material that uses only its remaining stock and is not ordered again. Left out of order alerts.
Planned departure · Shipment confirmedRecords of the departure date the supplier gave · the day it actually left (tracking no. · B/L).
Reorder dateExpected run-out date − lead time.
Planned receipt · Confirm receiptPlan for what will arrive · record of what actually arrived.
MESIn-house production · purchasing system. Its purchase data is the basis for actual receipts.
LedgerThe Finance & Accounting team's month-end stock ledger. The starting point of the stock calculation.
≈A value estimated, for example from an average, for a month with no data.

Privacy · Terms of Use

Bottom of the main screen · top left of the login screen

What personal information the system processes and why, and the rules users must follow, are written up separately. If you are new, please read them once.

  • Privacy Policy: information processed (name · employee number · department · position · work email · access log), purpose, retention period, outsourcing, rights of data subjects, contact (information security officer).
  • System Terms of Use: account · password management, prohibited actions, data download · taking data outside, access log, measures for violations.
  • Logins · access changes · key actions · file downloads are recorded for 1 year, with user · date/time · access location.
  • To delete your account, request it with Request account deletion in the 🔑 window. It is processed within 30 days.
